# Solo Leveling: Self-Improvement App

An epic self-improvement app inspired by the "Solo Leveling" anime, designed to turn your life into a multi-layered adventure.

## 🎯 Project Overview
This app gamifies personal growth across four essential pillars: Body, Soul, Mind, and Money. Using AI-driven tracking and RPG-style progression, users evolve from Rank E to Rank S in their real lives.

## 🔷 Core Pillars

### 1. 🏋️‍♂️ Body (Physical Development)
- **Training:** Daily quests (100 pushups, 100 situps, etc.).
- **AI Camera Tracking:** Real-time rep counting and form correction.
- **Progression:** Unlock professional sports (Boxing, Calisthenics, etc.) at higher ranks.

### 2. 🧘‍♂️ Soul (Psychological Balance)
- **Meditation:** Deep breathing and silence sessions.
- **Challenges:** "3 Days of Gratitude," "Negativity Fast."
- **AI Support:** Daily mood indicators and motivational quotes from anime personas.

### 3. 🧠 Mind (Intellectual Growth)
- **Cognitive Games:** Logic puzzles and focus challenges.
- **Gated Reading:** PDF library where reading progress is locked by AI-generated quizzes.
- **Tools:** Pomodoro timers and brain development metrics.

### 4. 💰 Money (Financial Literacy)
- **Education:** Summaries of financial classics (Rich Dad Poor Dad, etc.).
- **Management:** Virtual wallet and investment simulations.
- **Daily Quests:** Savings challenges and spending audits.

## 🎓 The Academy
A dedicated learning hub featuring courses in:
- Programming
- Graphic Design
- Freelancing
- Artificial Intelligence

## 🔥 Gamification Mechanics
- **Rank System:** E (Beginner) to S (Legendary).
- **Daily Dungeon:** A 10-minute multi-pillar challenge with epic rewards.
- **Hunter Market:** Buy AI personas, XP boosters, and avatar customizations.
- **Community:** A gated social feed with limited screen time to encourage real-world action.

## ⚙️ Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** Flutter
- **Backend:** Firebase (Firestore, Auth)
- **AI:** Custom rep-counting algorithms and persona-based LLMs.
