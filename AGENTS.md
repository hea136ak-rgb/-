# Agent Instructions: Solo Leveling App

## 🎨 UI/UX Guidelines
- **Theme:** Always use a dark, sleek "RPG" aesthetic.
- **Colors:** Deep blues, purples, and neon highlights.
- **Inspiration:** Refer to the "Solo Leveling Status" screen for HUD-style elements.
- **Animations:** Use high-energy transitions reminiscent of anime battle sequences.

## 🛠 Coding Conventions
- **Naming:** Use RPG terminology where appropriate (e.g., `LevelingService`, `DungeonController`, `HunterModel`).
- **Structure:** Modular architecture separating the four pillars.
- **Localization:** Support for Arabic (primary) and English.

## 🧠 Logical Directives
1. **The "Gatekeeper" Pattern:** Certain app features (like exiting a reading session) must be programmatically locked until a specific requirement (e.g., a quiz) is met.
2. **Progression Scaling:** Tasks must start simple (Rank E) and automatically increase in difficulty as the user's XP grows.
3. **Privacy:** Onboarding data (psychological profiling) must be handled with high sensitivity and encryption.

## 🤖 AI Personas
When implementing AI assistant features, the tone should match the chosen persona:
- **Goku:** High energy, encouragement through challenge.
- **Sung Jin-Woo:** Calm, strategic, and direct.
- **Ayanokoji:** Analytical and stoic.
